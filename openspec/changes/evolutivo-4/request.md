## Agreed scope summary (confirmed with user)

1. Logs: move out of Perfil into a dedicated "Logs" button/screen with search + date from/to filters, showing everything by default; layout with date/time on the left and description on the right.
2. Obligation cards: label/value layout with bold labels (Tipo, Inmueble, Proveedor, Responsabilidad, Cuenta, Monto estimado, Frecuencia, Aviso).
3. Obligation statuses: orange while within the current month up to and including the due day; "Vencido" in bold red after the due day. Remove confirm/forecast ("previsto") distinction. Add Edit, Delete and "Registrar pago" (expense-like form, prefilled, source account, payment method, optional receipt attachment) and the payment must show up in the asset history.
4. Asset history modal: rebuilt — month selector (one month or all since creation), grouped by month and obligation type, showing provider, type, month and paid/unpaid; click a record to see details; linked receipt opens in an iframe viewer with zoom. All the current content leaves this modal.
5. Home: remove "Movimiento entre cuentas" section; add "Transferencia" type in "Nueva transacción" (source account, destination account, amount, title, note) affecting account balances only, not income/expense/savings.

User instruction: implement everything end to end, then deploy (push to master triggers CI deploy to Fly.io) and notify.
