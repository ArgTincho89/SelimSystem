## Product defaults adopted (auto mode — to be confirmed by the user)

- **Q1 Cadastral**: new optional text field "Identificación catastral"; the obligations list previously under that heading moves to "Obligaciones del bien".
- **Q2 Sub-account balances**: shown per account and currency; children listed under the parent; no cross-currency sums; Home KPIs count each account once. A sub-account cannot be primary.
- **Q2b Assignment**: any income/expense/refund may use an account or sub-account. Pay-dialog ranking: titular accounts first, then their sub-accounts in the occurrence currency, then the rest. Sub-account inherits the parent's `ownerId` unless set.
- **Q2c Deletion**: a parent with active children cannot be archived/deleted; a child with movements is archived, not deleted.
- **Q3 IVA classification**: locación collection with IVA → `clasificacion:'A'` + `ivaPorcentaje`; without IVA stays unclassified (B). Editable in the collection dialog.
- **Q4 Occurrences per modality**: one expected occurrence per modality per period, labelled Locación/Comodato/Mutuo. Locación amount = original × (1 + IVA%).
- **Q5 Existing data**: legacy contracts migrate to one modality (`rental` → locación, `usufruct` → comodato); `condition` kept as derived; `ageYears` shown as "Antigüedad (dato anterior)".
- **Q6 Logs** (placement confirmed by user): a "LOGS" entry inside Perfil → "Acciones de cuenta" (`public/js/pages/profile.js:113,171`), listing all of the user's own actions newest first in plain Spanish (e.g. "Registraste el cobro del alquiler de Casa Centro, período mayo 2026") with day, hour and minute. Defaults: write actions only (POST/PUT/PATCH/DELETE) incl. failures; no request bodies/credentials; 12-month retention.
- **Q7 Money input**: formatted on blur, es-AR separators, currency precision; non-money numbers unchanged.
- **Q8 Details modal**: "Añadir titularidad" and "Finalizar titularidad hoy" removed; holders managed in "Editar".
- **Q9 Locked contract edit**: allowed; start-date change rejected only when a collected occurrence would fall outside the new range; unpaid occurrences regenerated; save always shows the inline warning.
- **Q10 Reverse collection**: allowed unless the account was reconciled after the collection date; audit entry keeps old amounts; attached receipts kept, flagged on the asset.

2. `sub-accounts` — model, validation, balances, selects, pay-dialog ranking.
