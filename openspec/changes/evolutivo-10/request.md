# Evolutivo 10 — Medio de pago, cheque and credit installments, alert lead-time configuration

> Branch: `evolutivo-10`. Delivery: PR to master (follow-up to evolutivo-9).

## Request (summary of the user's words)
- "Nueva transacción" opens scrolled down to "Título"; it must open at the top with the type selector visible.
- Rename "Medio de pago reutilizable" to "Medio de pago" and make it a fixed list: Transferencia, Efectivo, Débito, Crédito, Cheque.
- Cheque: split the amount in cuotas, each with its due date, amount and cheque number; each cuota stays pending until its imputation is confirmed, and Avisos reminds about it.
- Crédito: purchase in cuotas with an interest percentage; each cuota is charged on the purchase day of the following months, and Avisos announces the upcoming cuota.
- Configure the alert lead time per alert type, with specific exceptions (e.g. rent adjustment 15 days by default, 30 days for one property).

## Decisions
- D1: The transaction modal no longer focuses "Título": it focuses the active type button and resets every scroll container to 0 (new and edit).
- D2: "Medio de pago" is a fixed `<select>` (optional "Seleccionar" placeholder) stored in the existing `paymentMethod` text field with the exact values 'Transferencia' | 'Efectivo' | 'Débito' | 'Crédito' | 'Cheque'. Crédito only for Gasto; Cheque for Gasto and Ingreso. New writes are validated on the server; a legacy value is kept while unchanged and shown as an extra option when editing. The paymentMethods collection and routes stay (the form just stops using them).
- D3: Cheque (new Gasto/Ingreso): "Cuotas" 1–24 and one row per cuota with "Vencimiento cuota Nº x", "Monto" and "Nº cheque" (required, trimmed, max 30, letters/digits/dashes). Amounts are prefilled with an even split (last absorbs rounding) and must add up to Monto exactly. One POST /api/transactions with `chequeInstallments: [{ dueDate, amount, chequeNumber }]` validates everything before writing and creates one transaction per cuota: title "… — Cheque cuota x/N", `status: 'pending'`, `chequeGroupId`, `chequeInstallment: { number, total, dueDate, originalDate, chequeNumber, baseTitle }`. Recurrence is not available; attribution allows one asset (`attributionAssetId`, stored as a movement allocation per cuota); Mixto income is rejected.
- D4: `status: 'pending'` transactions are excluded from every total through `isPostedTransaction` (economic overview, legacy summary and trends, incomeByCurrency, account balances, IVA by holder). Missing status = posted. Home shows a "Pendiente de confirmación" badge and the cheque number. POST /api/transactions/:id/confirm and /unconfirm (reason required, UndoDialog) are logged in the audit log.
- D5: New alert `transaction_cheque_due` for pending cheque cuotas due within the lead (default 7; overdue always shown): "Cheque Nº 12345 — cuota 2/3 de «Título» vence el dd/mm/aaaa — pendiente de confirmar". Expense → owner; income attributed to an asset with an active rental contract with tenants → tenant; otherwise owner. Actions: "Confirmar imputación" and "Ver movimiento".
- D6: Crédito (new Gasto): "Cuotas" 1–36 and "Interés (%)" (≥ 0, decimals). total = Monto × (1 + i/100), split evenly in minor units (last absorbs rounding), live preview "N cuotas de $X (total $Y)". N = 1 is a normal expense with the interest applied. N > 1 creates N posted expenses on the purchase day of each following month (clamped to month end), title "«Título» — cuota x/N", `creditGroupId`, `creditInstallment: { number, total, interestPercent, purchaseDate, purchaseTitle, baseAmountMinor }`. The form no longer calls the credit-card plan API; existing plans keep working.
- D7: New alert `credit_installment_upcoming` for credit cuotas dated in (today, today + lead] (default 5): "Ingresará el cobro de la cuota Nº x/N del gasto «Título» realizado el dd/mm/aaaa ($monto)". Owner; action "Ver movimiento".
- D8: Centralized lead-time catalog in `src/services/alert-config.js` (label, description, default, kind lead/lookback/info, allowed scopes) stored per user in `alertSettings` `{ defaults, overrides: [{ id, type, scope, targetId, days }] }`. Resolution: obligation override > asset override > account override > user default > catalog default, through one `leadDaysFor(type, { assetId, obligationId, accountId })` used by every alert builder and by the account forecast. obligation_overdue and partial_payment are informational; document_pending uses lookback days (60). obligation.reminderLeadDays and plan.reminderLeadDays are no longer read (the obligation form drops "Aviso"); a customized accountSettings.alertLeadDays seeds rent_due and account_forecast on first read. GET/PUT /api/alerts/settings (days 0–365, type and scope validated, target must exist, one override per type+scope+target). Configuración > Avisos shows "Anticipación por tipo de aviso", "Excepciones específicas" with "Agregar excepción", and the "Avisos de saldo" subsection, saved with one "Guardar".
