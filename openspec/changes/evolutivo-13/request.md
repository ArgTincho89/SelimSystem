# Evolutivo 13 — Titular as tenant and automatic mirror expense

> Branch: `evolutivo-13`. Delivery: PR to master, merge after CI green, deploy (follow-up to evolutivo-12).

## Request (summary of the user's words)
- Settings > Inquilino > "Agregar inquilino": besides creating a new tenant, pick an existing titular so the same entity is both titular and inquilino (no duplicate). The same "pick existing" option should work for the other panels.
- Tenant pickers of rentals must list every tenant, including titulares that are tenants; a titular cannot rent its own asset.
- User decision "Gasto espejo automático": when a rent collection is registered and the contract tenant is a titular, automatically create the tenant titular's expense (same amount, currency, date and payment method) on an account of that titular chosen in the collection form, with the same IVA as credit VAT for that titular when the income line applies IVA.
- The mirror expense is read-only (like rent-linked incomes), it is undone with the collection, and both movements count in the totals.
- UI hints: the collection form says "Se registrará el gasto del inquilino <name> con su crédito fiscal"; Home shows "Gasto espejo del cobro de alquiler".

## Decisions
- D1: Entities stay one collection (`legalOwners` with `roles`). "Elegir existente" sends `PUT /api/owners/:id` with the panel role merged into the stored roles: same id, no new record. Source lists per panel: Inquilino ← titulares; Titular ← inquilinos and proveedores; Proveedor ← titulares and inquilinos. The option only shows when there are candidates and never while editing.
- D2: Self-rent is rejected in `rentals.validateContract` (shared by contracts, declarations and the asset form): a tenant that is a current holder of the asset (ownership without end date or ending today or later) answers 400 "<Nombre> es titular de este inmueble: un titular no puede alquilar su propio bien". Former holders may rent. The declaration picker hides current holders (already selected tenants stay listed).
- D3: Mirror link fields: the expense carries `mirrorOfTransactionId` (income id) and a dedicated `mirrorRentCollectionId` (not `rentCollectionId`, which every reader treats as a rent income); the collection stores `mirrorTransactionId`, and after undo `reversedMirrorTransactionId`.
- D4: The tenant account is per collection line (`lines[].tenantAccountId` in `POST /rentals/collections/month`, `tenantAccountId` in the legacy `POST /rentals/collections`), because lines may use different currencies. The form shows one "Cuenta del inquilino (titular)" select per line. Empty means the default: the tenant's primary active account in that currency, else the first by account order. A chosen account must be active, owned by the tenant titular and in the line currency. No account → 400 and nothing is written. The field joins the idempotency fingerprint only when sent (older replays keep their fingerprint).
- D5: With several titular tenants, the first titular in the contract `tenantIds` order pays. A tenant counts as titular when its roles include `owner`.
- D6: Mirror expense shape: tipo `expense`, amount and currency of the line, date = collection posting date (cheque → due date), payment method copied, titulo "Alquiler pagado — <asset> <MM/YYYY>", category "Alquiler" (case-insensitive) or null, clasificación `fijo`. IVA: same `ivaPorcentaje`/`ivaIncluded` as the income line when it applies IVA (A with IVA), with `ivaHolderId`/`ivaHolderName` = tenant titular, so `vatTotals` credits that titular. B lines carry no IVA. The legacy single collection mirrors the whole amount and only a fully A collection carries the IVA (a Mixto collection mirrors without IVA because an expense has no A/B split).
- D7: Everything is validated before writing; the mirror is written inside the existing all-or-nothing blocks (`atomically` for month collections, the manual rollback of the legacy route).
- D8: Mirror expenses are read-only: `PUT` and `DELETE /api/transactions/:id` answer 409 pointing to the rent collection. Undoing the collection (group, single or income DELETE) removes the mirror with its receipts, resets a reconciliation that included it, and a dependent movement on the mirror (refund, normalization, allocation) blocks the undo like on the income. The undo log adds "también se eliminó el gasto espejo del inquilino"; the collection log adds "con el gasto espejo de <name>".
- D9: Totals and per-holder views need no new code: the income keeps the landlord's holder snapshot and the expense lives on the tenant titular's account with its IVA holder. Month parts expose `tenantIds` so the collection form knows when to show the tenant account select.
